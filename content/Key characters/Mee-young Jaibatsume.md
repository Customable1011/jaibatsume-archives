Mee-young is the first daughter of [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] and first child of [[본가육신 Bonga Yuksin||The Main House Six]], she is the current head of [[The Family Council|the Jaibatsume family council]]; Mee-young was elevated into the position after the previous council lead, her grandfather [[Da-jung Jaibatsume]] left her the role in his will, something entirely unanticipated by the other councillors on the family.
