Kenzou was born of [[The Mawji Family]] and was arrange married into [[The Family Origins|the Jaibatsume family]] to [[Yumi Jaibatsume]].

He is the father to [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]], [[Hana Jaibatsume]], [[Eden Jaibatsume]], [[Ga-yoon Jaibatsume]], and [[Winter Celestiasume]]. Kenzou was a cruel, unforgiving and bitter man driven by ego and alcoholism; deeply unhappy with his life and his choices he took this out on [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] and [[Winter Celestiasume|Winter]] in the forms of physical and verbal abuse, often beating them or locking them in dark places where they would not be seen.

Later Kenzou would be stabbed and murdered using the [[Jaibatsume Relic]] at the hands of [[Winter Celestiasume|Winter]].
