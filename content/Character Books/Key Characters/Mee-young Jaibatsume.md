Mee-young is the first daughter of [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] and first child of [[본가육신 Bonga Yuksin|The Main House Six]], she is the current head of [[The Family Council|the Jaibatsume family council]]; Mee-young was elevated into the position after the previous council lead, her grandfather [[Da-jung Jaibatsume]] left her the role in his will, something entirely unanticipated by the other councillors on the family.

Mee-young has a condition called Dissociative Identity Disorder which causes her personality to fragment into various parts of herself, she was diagnosed at the **age of 16** when it was brought to her attention by her distant cousin [[Lia Mawji]].

Weirdly enough, she shares her dissociative disorder with her half-sister, [[Winter Celestiasume]], who also developed the disorder from shared abuse to their father, [[Kenzou Mawji]].
