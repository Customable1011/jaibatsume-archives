Yumi Jaibatsume is the mother and caretaker of the [[본가육신 Bonga Yuksin]], she inherited [[The Family Business|Jaibatsume Kimono Tailoring Services]] in 1998 following her first pregnancy and elevation to [[The Family Council]] and rebranded the business to **Harecuts**: Cosmetics and Accessories.

She was aware of the abuse [[Kenzou Mawji]] put her first daughter [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]] through to an extent but did not understand the full severity, she was pressured into silence from the rest of [[The Family Council|the Council]] to avoid any damage to the family reputation and the business reputation; Yumi Deeply regrets complying with this decision today after learning of the full extent of [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]]'s suffering.
