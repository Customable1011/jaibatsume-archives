Descending from a wealthy but disastrous family, Asami Hasegawa was raised in a strict environment as an **only child**, growing into the perfect daughter for others to envy. Following the arrest of one of her parents, she fell into **depression** at an early age and has carried the burden for over a decade. Alas, a curse was cast upon her at birth.  

﻿ After meeting [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]], the two quickly developed a romantic interest in each other and embraced a relationship that soon led to **marriage**, even though it wouldn't last more than three years. Asami, in an effort to keep her wife safe from a **malevolent entity**, chose to end their relationship to sever their bond and ensure [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] could live her life without worries. 

﻿ On the same day, her mother passed away, followed by her father a week later due to unfortunate circumstances, leading to her adoption by the **Saiky** family and becoming Sho and Koizumi's **daughter**, thus becoming an effective member of the House of Knowledge. The trauma endured in her hometown, combined with the loss of loved ones in Karakura, resurfaced her **post-traumatic stress disorder**, which was only diagnosed in adulthood due to her worsening symptoms.
