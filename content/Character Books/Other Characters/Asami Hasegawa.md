Descending from a wealthy but disastrous family, Asami Hasegawa was raised in a strict environment as an **only child**, grown into becoming the perfect child for others to envy. In succession to the arrest of one of her parents, she fell into **depression** into an early age, and currently carrying the curse with her for over a decade. Alas, an actual curse was cast upon birth. 

After meeting [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]], the two quickly developed a romantic interest for each other and embraced a relationship that soon brought the both of them to **marry**, even though it wouldn't have lasted more than three years. Asami, in an effort to keep her wife safe from a **malevolent entity**, chose to end their relationship to cut their string and ensure she could live her life without worries. 

On the same day, her mother passed away, followed by her father a week later due to unfortunate circumstances, leading to her adoption by the **Saiky** family and becoming Sho and Koizumi's **daughter**, becoming an effective member of the House of Knowledge. The trauma endured in her hometown, and the loss of loved ones in Karakura resurfaced her **post-traumatic stress disorder** that had only been diagnosed into adult age, due to her symptoms worsening.
