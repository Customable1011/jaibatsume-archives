Born as **Dionysus Edgar Jaccard Mawj-Gim III Jr III** to [[Sakura Mawji]] and [[Dae-Hyung Gim]], Den Mawji was raised on the Jaibatsume estate by [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] and [[Kenzou Mawji]]. A true mama’s boy, he was practically glued to Yumi’s side during his early years, learning basic housekeeping skills like **cleaning**, **gardening**, **cooking**, and **baking**.

As he grew, Den developed a mischievous streak, often getting into trouble on the streets of Fukui with his groundskeeper’s child, **Kadie**. His rebellious nature only intensified after discovering [[Kenzou Mawji|Kenzou]]’s abuse of his “*sisters*.” In an effort to protect them, Den made himself a target, deliberately provoking [[Kenzou Mawji|Kenzou]] to redirect his violent outbursts onto him instead. Witnessing [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]]’s mental deterioration firsthand - especially the emergence of one of her alter's, **Kimiko** - Den felt responsible for her safety; when [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] left to start fresh in Karakura, Den followed, determined to watch over her.

However, the move left him feeling lost, especially as [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] made it clear that his presence was more of a burden than a comfort. After enduring years of chaos caused by [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] and [[Asami Hasegawa|her terrible decisions]], an embittered Den began to distance himself. In an act of self-reliance, he forged a new sense of family outside the Jaibatsume name, determined to carve out an identity of his own.
