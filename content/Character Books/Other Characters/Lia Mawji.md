Lia was born to [[Sakura Mawji]] and [[Dae-Hyung Gim]] as the eldest sibling, her younger brother [[Dae-Hyun Mawji ( Den )|Den]] arriving 18 months later. They were both left in their mother's custody following their parents divorce but [[Sakura Mawji|Sakura]] soon fell into alcoholism and became abusive. Lia shielded [[Dae-Hyun Mawji ( Den )|Den]] as long as she could from the abuse their mother put them through, eventually [[Dae-Hyun Mawji ( Den )|Den]] was taken into custody by [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] who opted to look after the child in [[Sakura Mawji|Sakura]]'s 'innocent plea for help' which left them separated, Lia under the continued care of her own abusive mother; Lia spent the next eight years making multiple attempts at escaping her mother and one day, at the age of 13, she succeeded, finding herself in [[Karakura]].

By chance, Lia befriended [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]] but quickly took notice to the strange shifts in her demeanour, often accompanied by gaps in her memory; she brought this odd behaviour up to [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] which ultimately lead to her diagnosis of Dissociative Identity Disorder. Over time, Lia took on the leadership role of the [[The Celestial Family|Celestial Family]], a responsibility entrusted to her by [[Winter Celestiasume]]; fiercely protective of those she considers family, she has clashed with [[Mee-young Jaibatsume|Mee-young]] on multiple occasions and has even put herself in harm's way for the sake of her family.

Beyond her family ties, Lia is a passionate - if slightly obsessive, young woman with a deep interest in the **occult** and **journalism**. Her love for broadcasting led her to join Karakura High School’s radio show as a co-host alongside Mee-Young. Over time, she took over as the show's main host, cementing her voice in Karakura’s media scene.
