The Jaibatsume Relic was a katana handed down generations through the family and used for ceremonial practice, usually for trials performed to elevated family onto positions on the council, later replaced with the [[Rifle and Six shots ceremony]], the blade was used to slice six tatami mats in under 15 seconds as a demonstration of commitment, strength and will power.

The blade was later passed down to [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] and was used by [[Winter Celestiasume]] to execute [[Kenzou Mawji]]; it was passed onto [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]] who was later stabbed with the blade by her Ex-wife, [[Asami Hasegawa]] in a deceitful moment with a dangerous entity.

Today the blade lies in the hands of [[Tamashi Tankyu-sha]] who “elevated such a cursed relic from the hands of those fat enforcers”
