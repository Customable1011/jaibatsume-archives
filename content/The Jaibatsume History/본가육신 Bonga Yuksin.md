The Six Spirits of the main house are the six daughters of [[Yumi Jaibatsume]], all of which who resided on the Central Jaibatsume Estate during the 21st Century. The six consisted of:

* [[Mee-young Jaibatsume]]
* [[Hana Jaibatsume]]
* [[Eden Jaibatsume]]
* [[Ga-yoon Jaibatsume]]
* [[Ena Jaibatsume]]
* [[Jade Jaibatsume]]

Whilst they are considered the main six children of the house, [[Dae-Hyun Mawji ( Den )]] was also fostered by [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] and cared for as though they were one of the main six.

[[Ena Jaibatsume|Ena (Tanaka)]] and [[Jade Jaibatsume|Jade (???)]] Are both adopted into the family.

[[The Family Council]] considered it a blessing of purity and a sign sent by the deities that [[Yumi Jaibatsume]] had only daughters birthed; they did not take to kindly to her ‘*tainting*’ such a blessing by taking on the responsibility of [[Dae-Hyun Mawji ( Den )]]. They kept their thoughts to themselves, unable to argue [[Yumi Jaibatsume|Yumi]]’s decision with how much of the family assets she owned.
