In 1952, The Jaibatsume family founded a small business in Fukui, Jaibatsume's Kimono Tailoring Services; the foundation of the business grew popular over the years and opened many other branches across all of Japan as technology evolved, drawing in customers from all over, eventually compelling them to rebrand under the Harecuts name in 1999, providing not only accessories and apparel but also cosmetic services.

Today, a Harecuts branch, rebranded under the name Jaibatsume Cosmetology, managed by the daughter of the current owner of Harecuts, has found itself in Karakura merging with its partners at Celestial Incorporate to bring you the ultimate tailoring and ordering experience, featuring custom-made ordering systems and new outlooks on your experience with tailoring companies
